Welcome to War of the Guardians ("we," "our," or "us"). To provide an optimal cross-platform multiplayer experience, we may collect the following categories of data:

  • Account Identifiers: Platform-specific user IDs (Meta Horizon ID, Steam ID, Apple ID) used for authentication and multiplayer matchmaking.
  • VR Telemetry & Motion Data: Anonymized headset and controller tracking data strictly necessary for avatar positioning, combat synchronization, and locomotion calibration.
  • In-Game Activity & Progress: Character stats, inventory items, quest progression, guild memberships, and in-game chat logs.
  • Technical & Diagnostic Data: Crash reports, frame rate metrics, and hardware configuration to diagnose bugs and improve game performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected data solely to:

  • Facilitate cross-platform multiplayer gameplay and matchmaking.

  • Provide technical support and respond to player inquiries.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared with platform providers (Meta, Valve, Apple) only to the extent required to authenticate your login, process authorized in-game purchases, and enable cross-play infrastructure.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

You may request the deletion of your account and associated game data at any time by contacting our support team. Upon verification, your account data will be permanently purged within 30 days, subject to legal retention obligations.
